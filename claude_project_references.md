## 1. Target Venue

**Let's target a Springer Conference Proceedings (specifically the LNCS - Lecture Notes in Computer Science format).** 

Realistically, for an academic practicum project, a conference is the best fit. Journals often have 6–12 month review cycles, whereas conferences are quicker and better suited for applied systems.
